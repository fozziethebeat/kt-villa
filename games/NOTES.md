# Other Games

## Connecting, Accepting, Reflecting

A three part game series that tells a story of one family
1. Connecting: A man gets disconnected from his family in the regular world and sent to a final fantasy like world.  He finds allies and gains the power of the crystals to bring the to worlds toegther.  At the end he sacrifices himself just upon entering the regular world in order to save his wife.
2. Accepting: The man's wife is now given the powers of the crystals but doesn't believe in herself.  She has to catch up and gain both trust and faith in the man's crystal warrior friends in order to repair the blended world that has merged two realities that seperated long ago.  At the end, she again sacrifies herself to protect her young daughter.
3. Reflecting: The young daughter inherited the power from her father passed to her mother and now her.  She's fully accepted what happened and the power that comes with it and thus is able to easily deal with the monsters that still roam the world but she's powerless when trying to look back at what happened and actually reflecting.  In the third world, the mental world, she has to fight alongside her parents to truly connect, accept, and reflect on who she is, what made her, and her responsibility moving forward.
