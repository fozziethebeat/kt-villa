# Notes on Other Games

## Connecting, Accepting, Reflecting

A three part game series that tells a story of one family
1. Connecting: A man gets disconnected from his family in the regular world and
   sent to a final fantasy like world.  He finds allies and gains the power of
the crystals to bring the to worlds together.  At the end he sacrifices himself
just upon entering the regular world in order to save his wife.
2. Accepting: The man's wife is now given the powers of the crystals but
   doesn't believe in herself.  She has to catch up and gain both trust and
faith in the man's crystal warrior friends in order to repair the blended world
that has merged two realities that separated long ago.  At the end, she again
sacrifice herself to protect her young daughter.
3. Reflecting: The young daughter inherited the power from her father passed to
   her mother and now her.  She's fully accepted what happened and the power
that comes with it and thus is able to easily deal with the monsters that still
roam the world but she's powerless when trying to look back at what happened
and actually reflecting.  In the third world, the mental world, she has to
fight alongside her parents to truly connect, accept, and reflect on who she
is, what made her, and her responsibility moving forward.

## Spirit Farer

* A builder game
* Time economy, everything takes a certain amount of time and some actions can
  only be taken at certain times of the day
* A happiness mechanic, need to meet the needs of the people on the boat with
  different preferences
* Item economy needed to ration building on the boat and feeding the people
* Automation Economy by adding new people on the boat they enable new actions
  and can automate some actions
* Gradual exploration by finding new people or solving quests.

## Spirit Scout

* A quest driven game that gradually exposes more of the island to be explored
* No action or time economy so far
* A small finding money economy for buying things

## Tiny Town

* A builder game
* An action economy, each action fills up your time bar that eventually ends
  the day
* A basic economy of selling or finding money that enables buying things that
  facilitating building more in the game
* Gradual exploration by investing in the the travel building
* A sort of romance mechanic with complimenting and hanging out with village
  members and a leveling system
* Leveling system for skills that unlocks new areas

## World Computer

* A pure multi-level puzzle game
* A "computer" simulator that contains hints or clues for the underlying "game"
  simulator
* Gradual exploration by finding items that fix broken elements in the game
* A sort of friend mechanic with some bots being notable NPCs

## Is this seat taken?

* Simple constraint satisfaction puzzle game
* N people to be placed
* Eacn person has one or more facts or constraints that limits where they can be placed
* Levels get progressively harder by seat layout, number of people, and the constraints
