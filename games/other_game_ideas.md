# Other Game Ideas

## Rogue Like Mountain Snow Cleaning

You own a single small snow cleaning machine and have to work to clean the ski resort mountains overnight throughout the ski season
* Make bids on which mountain you'll tackle
* Prove your skills on the simple mountains first before anyone will even consider your bids on the harder mountains
* Each run changes dynamically as the weather changes and the resort guests mess things up
* build out your rig with ways to make cleaning easier or cooler
* Off season has two big options
  * Switch to being a farmer in the hot summer
  * Go down under to australia where you can't understand anyone and everything is upside down
* The rig is part of the build system which you can slowly upgrade to either be fast and nimble or big and beefy
