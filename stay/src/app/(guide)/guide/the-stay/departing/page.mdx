# Departing & Checkout Checklist

Before leaving KT Villa, please tidy up the rooms, drop off trash, and shut down the heating and water systems that were turned on when you arrived. This prevents frozen burst pipes while the house is unoccupied in sub-zero weather.

## 1. Recharge the Car Jump-Starter

The day before you leave, make sure the portable car battery jump-starter is **fully charged** and placed back on the living room bookcase for the next guests.

![Car Charger](/img/checkout/checkout-car-charger.png)

## 2. Cleaning, Laundry & Trash Drop-Off

Before heading out, please complete these four steps:

- **Vacuum the floors lightly** (vacuum is in the living room; sprays and cleaning towels are under the kitchen sink)
- **Wash & dry used bedsheets** (or run them at the Coin Laundry near Aeon if short on time)
- **Drop off sorted trash** at Green Valley Hakuba depot down the street and line bins with fresh 45L (0.025mm) polymer bags
- **Return furniture, dishes, and linens** to their standard positions shown below

![Under-sink cleaning supplies](/img/checkout/checkout-kitchen-left-01.png)

## 4. Turning Down the House & Draining Water

This is the exact reverse of [Arriving & Warming Up the House](/guide/the-stay/arriving#3-warming-up-the-house--water):

1. **Turn off the hot water heater**
2. **Turn off the water pump and press the gray drain button**
3. **Turn off the main gas heating panel and all brown heater boxes**

<Callout type="warning" title="Turn Off All Heater Boxes & 2F AC">
  Double-check that the A/C unit on the second floor and every individual brown room heater box on the first floor are switched completely off.
</Callout>

When properly shut down, the heating and water pump panels should look like these photos:

![Heating panel turned off](/img/IMG_3999.png)

![Water pump turned off](/img/IMG_4002.png)

On the water pump controller, press the **off button** and then press the **large gray drain button** so the green indicator light turns off — this drains standing water so the pipes don't freeze and burst.

Finally, leave the garage remote on the breadbox inside the garage, lock the front door, and secure the house keys back inside the exterior lockbox!
