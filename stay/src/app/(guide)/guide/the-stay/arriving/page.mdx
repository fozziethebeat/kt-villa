# Arriving & Warming Up the House

Once you arrive at KT Villa, follow these steps to unlock the house, get the car ready if needed, and turn on the heating and water systems.

## 1. The Keys and Doors

On the primary front-facing door, there is a wooden shelf at the top of the stairs. Attached to the shelf is a **key lockbox**. Enter the code (provided by Keith) and you will find two sets of door keys inside.

Additionally, inside the garage there is a **garage door opener** located near the door on top of a small breadbox. You can use this to enter and exit through the garage.

![The garage opener](/img/IMG_4008.png)

## 2. Getting the Car Started

Depending on when in the season you arrive, the car battery may need a jump. Find the portable car jump-starter (on the living room bookshelf) and connect the colored clamps to the matching battery terminals.

![Car Charger](/img/checkout/checkout-car-charger.png)

## 3. Warming Up the House & Water

When no one is in the house for a few days, three systems are turned off to prevent frozen pipes:

1. **The main gas heating panel**
2. **The water pump**
3. **The hot water heater**

Turn all three on when you arrive so the house warms up and running water is restored.

![The heating system](/img/IMG_3998.png)

This is the main heating control panel. **Turn on all three buttons** to activate the gas heating system, and leave the thermostat dial where it is.

<Callout type="danger" title="Important: Thermostat Dial">
  Do **not** set the thermostat dial to `6` — the heating circulation stops working at that setting. Leave the temperature dial untouched.
</Callout>

Next, turn on one or more of the individual room heater boxes to circulate warm air into the rooms:

![A heater](/img/IMG_4005.png)

On each brown heater box, you can set the fan strength and heat level independently.

![The water pump](/img/IMG_4001.png)

Turn on this **general water pump switch** so water flows to all faucets and showers.

![The water heater](/img/IMG_4003.png)

Finally, turn on the **hot water heater panel** so you can enjoy hot showers and warm water at the sinks.
