# Rooms & Amenities

Please treat the lodge with the **campfire rule**: leave rooms and gear even cleaner and better organized than you found them.

## The Bedrooms

KT Villa has three bedrooms:

- **1F Tatami Room**: Primary guest bedroom (fits up to 3 guests with futons)
- **2F Office Guest Room**: Second guest room (has two standing desks, a spare half-bath, closet, and a sofa bed)
- **1F Bedroom (Keith & Tianyi's Room)**: Private owner room near the washing machine — please do not use unless explicitly approved

<Callout type="danger" title="Do Not Lock Bedroom Doors">
  We do not have spare keys for the interior bedroom doors! Please **never lock bedroom doors**, otherwise someone may get locked out and require drilling the lock.
</Callout>

## Kitchen, Espresso & Projector

Feel free to cook in the kitchen and return all dishes, pots, and utensils to their original spots when done.

<Callout type="danger" title="Wooden Counters Are Not Cutting Boards">
  Please **never cut directly on the wooden kitchen countertops**! Always use a cutting board.
</Callout>

![The Espresso Machine](/img/IMG_4004.png)

Help yourself to the **espresso machine** with any coffee beans you bring along.

![The Advanced Projector](/img/IMG_4023.png)

The living room roll-down curtain doubles as a screen for the **projector** — plug in your laptop over HDMI for movie nights.

## Driveway Parking & Snowblower

![The driveway](/img/IMG_4011.png)

**Driveway Parking Tip:** Never park directly in front of the garage door or under the roof overhang, as heavy snow slides off the roof! Park behind the line formed by the exterior metal stairs (fits two cars safely).

![The snowblower](/img/IMG_4010.png)

If snowfall is heavy, there is a small gas snowblower in front of the garage (the key is stored near the garage door opener).
